PHILOSOPHY

How OverKill Hill thinks

The “OverKill” in the name isn’t about complexity for its own sake. It’s about going far enough into the details that you trust the system when everything is moving fast.

Protocol-driven by default

We design AI interactions as protocols, not one-off prompts. Clear steps, defined states and escape hatches for when reality doesn’t match the diagram.

Performance forged, not presumed

Every system gets road-tested with edge cases, weird inputs and real-world mess. If it breaks, we reforge it. We don’t blame the user.

Crafted to scale with humans

The goal is not “replace the human.” It’s to build tools that help ambitious people think more clearly, move faster and feel less alone in the complexity.
