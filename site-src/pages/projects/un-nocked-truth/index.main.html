About Un‑NOCKed Truth

Archery has historically been the province of elites and warriors. Un‑NOCKed Truth aims to lower the barrier to entry. The concept combines safety tutorials, discipline coaching and meditative storytelling to help anyone pick up a bow and find flow.

The proposed GPTs would explore questions about form, equipment and history. Availability and testing are not established on this page.

Proposed GPTs

  • Form Coach: Would provide step‑by‑step guidance on stance, draw and release, with metaphors drawn from dance and yoga.
  • Equipment Matchmaker: Would help you choose the right bow, arrows and accessories based on your goals and body.
  • Safety Sentinel: Would answer safety questions and reinforces proper range protocol.
  • Archery Historian: Would tell stories of archery in different cultures and epochs to deepen your connection.
