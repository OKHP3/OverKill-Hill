PathScrib‑R

A concept for a narrative copilot and generative writing suite. Explore, draft and iterate on your stories with AI assistance.

About PathScrib‑R

PathScrib‑R is planned for authors, game masters and world builders. It would help you brainstorm plot points, refine characters and experiment with narrative structure. A proposed Copilot Agent would let you delegate tasks like outlining, summarizing and even converting text into storyboards.

Planned Copilot & GPT Components

  • Plot Weaver: Would suggest possible turns and twists based on your genre and themes.
  • Character Smith: Would generate nuanced character sheets and backstories.
  • Arc visualizer: Would convert a synopsis into a simple plot arc diagram.
  • Copilot Agent: A proposed multi‑step agent that would take user instructions and execute them across the PathScrib‑R suite.
