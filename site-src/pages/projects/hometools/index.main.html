About Homestead‑R

Homestead‑R is planned to support modern homesteaders and small farmers with AI‑powered planning and advice. Whether you’re managing a backyard garden or a regenerative farm, the proposed GPTs would help you plan crop rotations, monitor soil health and keep your animals healthy.

Planned GPTs

  • Crop Planner: Would suggest what to plant, when and where based on your climate zone and goals.
  • Soil Analyst: Would interpret soil test results and recommend amendments.
  • Livestock Tracker: Would keep records of feeding, health and breeding for your animals.
  • Disaster Prep Coach: Would help you prepare for weather events and supply disruptions with checklists and guides.
