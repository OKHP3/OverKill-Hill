About Homestead‑R

Homestead‑R is a concept for supporting modern homesteaders and small farmers with AI-assisted planning. Proposed tools would help organize crop rotations, soil information and animal-care records for a backyard garden or a regenerative farm.

Proposed GPTs

  • Crop Planner: Would suggest what to plant, when and where based on your climate zone and goals.
  • Soil Analyst: Would interpret soil test results and recommends amendments.
  • Livestock Tracker: Would keep records of feeding, health and breeding for your animals.
  • Disaster Prep Coach: Would help you prepare for weather events and supply disruptions with checklists and guides.
