## Canonical relationships

### 1. Locked canon and living application

The parent manifesto and archive establish a two-layer model:

- **Locked layer:** origin, generational record, MurderBird sigil provenance, and the meaning of the visual grammar.
- **Living layer:** the 12-principle manifesto, future writing, posture states, material treatments, scene compositions, and new applications.

The supplied blueprint/CRT renders belong to the living application layer while expressing several locked visual ideas: blueprint as protocol, obsolete hardware as repurposed interface, mechanical construction as disassembly and reconstruction, controlled orange warmth, and an occupying rather than decorative posture.

### 2. P3 has two canonical meanings

- **Brand meaning:** Precision, Protocol, Promptcraft.
- **Method meaning:** Protocol-Driven Power Prompts.

The exponent is also treated as cubic depth. It represents layered perception, process, and protocol rather than three additive words. Future artifacts should preserve both meanings instead of collapsing P3 into either a tagline or a prompt-writing technique.

### 3. The MurderBird is a compressed system argument

The Notion pages repeatedly describe the bird as more than a logo. Its visual components carry system meaning:

- terror-bird silhouette: feral attention and refusal to let go;
- mechanical body: tinkering, disassembly, and reconstruction;
- CRT and exposed wiring: old interface repurposed into a new system;
- blueprint grid and stripes: structured, layered, categorical thought;
- orange eye and monitor: controlled warmth and signal;
- talons gripping hardware: occupation, pressure, and active investigation.

This supports the local image extracts, but it does not prove that every current render satisfies later material or transparent-subject requirements.
