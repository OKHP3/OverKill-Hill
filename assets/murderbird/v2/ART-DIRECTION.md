# MurderBird v2 — production direction

This library is an authored visual proposal for the expanded origin story at revision `2d7e92d23fcbe875c7421d4509109bffd22b0c48`. The user requested a site-wide image and rich-media asset array after identifying that earlier clips made the MurderBird appear hawk-sized. Approximately 1.8 m is the working adult-height target, not a newly published story fact.

## What stays recognisable

Deep hooked bill; swept, segmented crown; orange circular optic in the modern era; hand-worked bronze; the refitted left shoulder; long load-bearing legs; compact ornamental wings. The bird persists through repair rather than becoming a different polished robot. Its purpose is attentive problem-solving. Keep ordinary people, doorways, furniture and floor contact as scale evidence. Never enlarge the computer to rescue a perching composition.

These generated illustrations preserve a visual family, not a mechanically identical 3D rig. Small joint, pin, tail and plate differences remain. Use the Blender scale stage for future camera/prop planning; it is not an animation-ready character model.

## Era boundaries

| Beat | Materials and agency | Exclude |
|---|---|---|
| Maker | Hand-worked bronze, original mechanical linkages; court inspection | Complete fossil skull, modern wires, Victorian repair hardware |
| Water | Brown-black bronze and mineral seams, inert body | Active eyes, deliberate motion, later repairs |
| Recovery, 1853 | Inert corroded body, timber and rope load support | Modern lifting machinery or electronics |
| Mechanic, 1873 | Visible iron braces, brass bearings, external power | Autonomous modern mind, hidden self-contained power |
| Heart, 2025 | Ceramic power core, capacitor storage, old load paths | Magical reactor or an unexplained floating power system |
| First choice | Sensed instability and unassisted balance adjustment | A human guiding the decision or flight |
| Sentinel | Quiet attention beside the CRT | Computer casing bearing body weight |

The fictional fossils are leg/joint fragments; the bird's bill and courtly wings are design choices, not a fossil skull reconstruction. The bones are not incorporated into the machine.

## Website use

Use the negative-space hero for headline overlays; keep text as HTML. Narrative masters are available for chapter illustrations and editorial cards. Social layouts are separate compositions with accessible, legible type. Alt text describes what is visible; captions can explain story context. Decorative repeated artwork should have empty alt text where adjacent content supplies the same information.

Do not use the full-body silhouette as a tiny favicon. Existing vector/head icons and historical manifesto artwork have separate roles. Locale pages should share the same selected imagery, with translated surrounding copy and alt text rather than English text baked into artwork.
