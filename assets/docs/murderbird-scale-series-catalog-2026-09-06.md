Status: Seven source studies generated. Six opaque review candidates and one rejected transparency attempt. No site integration, favicon exports, video, sound, or 3D model delivered in this pass.

## Direction

Working height is approximately 2 meters, provisional pending owner review. Floor supports the bird; an ordinary CRT on a normal-height workbench establishes scale. Current story controls materials and three-era construction; older perch prompts are not production instructions. OverKill Hill brand profile 1.1.0 influenced dark espresso, patinated bronze and restrained orange optics. Built-in image generation used; no exact model version exposed. Blender installation was verified by a worker but not used. Two workers supplied consumer mapping and continuity review.
