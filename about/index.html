About OverKill Hill P³™

OverKill Hill P³™ is where design, precision, and automation converge: between the idea and the system that makes it real, between “we should automate this” and “we trust what we’ve built together.”

Explore the projects Back to the forge

Who’s on the Hill

At OverKill Hill P³™, I specialize in designing AI systems built to hold under real-world pressure: local inference stacks, agentic workflows, multi-model coordination, and the governance layer that makes it repeatable. My approach combines deep enterprise experience with hands-on AI architecture to deliver solutions that are innovative, practical, and built to outlast the platform they started on.

Founded by Jamie Hill, Lead Enterprise Architect with 13+ years of enterprise experience across complex systems, Salesforce architecture, and AI strategy. A career built on solving high-stakes problems at scale: from deep operational complexity into intelligent automation. That depth of real-world implementation experience is the defining differentiator that makes OverKill Hill P³™ systems grounded in real-world stakes, not just technical possibility.

I understand that every business is unique, and I pride myself on the ability to listen, learn, and adapt solutions to fit the distinct challenges faced by each client. Whether you're looking to streamline operations, enhance decision-making, or create entirely new workflows, OverKill Hill P³™ is here to help you harness the power of AI in a way that makes sense for your business.

Why the Hill exists

OverKill Hill P³™ exists to build AI systems that can shoulder real responsibility in complex environments. Too often, AI tools are more about flashy features than practical impact. I believe that AI should be a dependable partner that helps you get things done, not another source of uncertainty.

This is the place for people who don’t want “just a bot,” but a system that can carry part of the load without adding more chaos. The work is part engineering, part storytelling, part quietly protecting your future self from avoidable headaches. It’s about going beyond surface-level solutions to create tools that are robust, reliable, and ready for the real world.

How we work with you

Projects usually start with a conversation: context, constraints, the actual stakes. From there I sketch out the protocols, build a prototype, and then iterate until it behaves the way your world needs it to.

Sometimes that looks like a single, sharp-edged tool. Sometimes it’s a whole stack that touches multiple roles or departments. Either way, it’s built to last. I document everything, test for edge cases, and make sure you have what you need to maintain and evolve the system over time.

Ready to talk? Reach out at contact@overkillhill.com and I’ll start sketching. No pressure, no hard sells, just a conversation about what you’re building and how I might help.
